## 3. Academic Integrity and Invariant Compliance

1. **Number of Research Gaps:** Exactly **FIVE** critical research and engineering gaps are synthesized in the Literature Review (Section 2.8) and reflected consistently in the Abstract.
2. **Cryptographic Ledger vs. Legal Chain of Custody:** The paper accurately claims that ANVESH maintains a "cryptographically verifiable evidence ledger" via SHA-256 digests. It explicitly refrains from claiming that a software hash ledger by itself establishes an immutable legal chain of custody or proves the identity of human creators.
3. **Byte-Level Evidential Integrity:** SHA-256 is described as establishing that the "examined artifact remains byte-for-byte consistent with the artifact associated with the recorded hash," avoiding absolute or unscientific claims of "indisputable proof."
4. **Attribution Boundary Safeguard:** The invariant `Actor Identity: NOT ESTABLISHED` is strictly enforced. The platform is described as identifying and correlating infrastructure-level observables (IPs, ASNs, routing hops), explicitly denying claims of identifying human attackers.
5. **Zero Fabrication:** No experimental accuracies, F1-scores, precision, recall, or benchmark percentages are claimed. The research paper is properly framed as an MTE-stage working draft covering the completed Introduction and Literature Review.
